    what it is

    Material's structure, lit and moved like Apple glass

    Content sits on solid Material tonal surfaces. The controls floating above it are liquid glass: it bends everything behind it, text included, keeps what is under it legible, and catches one light.

    Drawn from embedded wall-panel dashboards: tonal tiles, pill sliders, lowercase mono labels and one warm signal colour. Motion comes from Detent's engine and patterns. Numbers are sourced, or marked as our own tuning.

    the rules

    • Glass is for controlsOnly the floating layer is glass; content stays on tonal surfaces. Never glass on glass. apple hig · materials
    • Glass bends everythingText, icons and surfaces under glass are refracted at its rim; nothing sits on top pretending to be behind it. wwdc25 · meet liquid glass
    • Regular glass stays legibleIt blurs and evens out the brightness behind it. Clear glass stays transparent and dims bright media by 35%. apple hig · materials
    • One lightA thin line of light on the cut edge, brightest facing one light direction that the pointer or the device's tilt leans. wwdc25 · meet liquid glass
    • Glass is liquid, within its groupPressed glass flexes and glows from the finger; shapes in one group melt together, as in a glass container; separate groups never touch. wwdc25 · meet liquid glass
    • Chrome steps backOn home and standby the dock tucks away after a few idle seconds and while you scroll down; any touch brings it back. bezel
    • Messages arrive from the topConfirmations grow out of a small island at the top edge and shrink back into it, well clear of the controls. detent · dynamic island morph
    • Fills carry their inkWhere the light slider fills under its label, the label takes the fill's on-colour exactly at the edge. Room dimmers fill with a quiet tonal container instead, so their words never split. m3 · color roles
    • Apps come from their iconsAn app grows out of the icon you touched and shrinks back into it; launcher icons cascade in from where you arrived. apple hig · motion
    • Faces keep room for actionsEach standby face says where its actions may sit, and the actions glide there as you page between faces, so nothing covers a clock. bezel
    • Everything can be caughtApps, the control center and the activity stack track the finger and can be caught mid-flight; letting go hands your velocity to a spring. detent · patterns
    • Live activities floatWhat is running lives in a small glass stack you can throw to any corner, the picture-in-picture pattern; it steps back while the control center is open. wwdc18 · designing fluid interfaces
    • The control center steps the page backPulling down dims and desaturates what is behind; its modules cascade in a beat apart and keep far enough apart that their glass never merges. apple hig · materials
    • Concentric cornersInner radius = outer radius − padding; a capsule's radius is half its height; a fill takes its track's outer corners. wwdc25 · new design system
    • Two voicesGoogle Sans Flex for what you read at a glance; Google Sans Code, lowercase, for quiet labels. bezel
    • One signalOrange marks the primary action and heat. Ice fills tonal controls and cooling; amber means a light is on. m3 · color roles
    • Motion from DetentPresses answer on pointer-down; drags track 1:1; releases hand their velocity to a sourced spring; flings coast and resist at the edges; dials click between detents. detent · patterns
    • A calm versionReduced transparency makes glass solid with a rim; reduced motion removes overshoot, drift and parallax. apple hig · accessibility

    Colour · current panel tone

    Dynamic colour · Monet

    Choose Cover or a basic colour and the panel takes its palette from one seed, as Android's Monet does. The seed is scored from the album art after a 128-colour quantizer, grown into HCT tonal palettes, and every role reads a fixed tone, so contrast holds whatever the seed.
